## 關於計畫
sodagreen academy（蘇打綠學院）是由 Miyun 於 2024 年 11 月透過 Threads 公開的一支 Line 官方帳號。
其主要為同年年底蘇打綠最新一場巡演 二十年一刻 臺北站搶票所建置的非官方題庫。
服務上線後於隔年二月底達 2300 位使用者，且截止 2026 年 9 月，仍有使用者使用該服務或提供題目。
考量 Line 官方帳號不利於長期營運與維護，便打算將該服務搬遷至網站上。

除題庫外，本計畫亦期望透過整理蘇打綠自 2001 年成團以來能在網路上公開找到的演出影像、雜誌專訪等數位資料，解決現有播放清單過於凌亂的困境。

## About the Project

**sodagreen academy** is an unofficial LINE Official Account launched by Miyun on Threads in November 2024.
Originally developed as an unofficial quiz bank to help fans prepare for ticket purchases for *二十年一刻* , Sodagreen's latest concert tour at the time, specifically its Taipei stop in late 2024, the service reached 2,300 users by the end of February 2025. As of September 2026, fans are still using the service and contributing new questions.
However, recognizing the limitations of LINE Official Accounts in terms of long-term operation and maintenance, this project aims to migrate the existing service to a dedicated website.

Beyond the quiz bank, the project also seeks to build a digital archive of publicly accessible materials related to Sodagreen since the band's formation in 2001. These materials include live performance recordings, magazine interviews, and other digital content, with the goal of addressing the fragmentation and disorganization of existing playlists and online collections.
